# Bitlads Software

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective customers and partners assessing the company's shipped applications and its software development work.

## Product Purpose

The company site presents its portfolio and gives visitors a path to discuss a project. The homepage features Book a Rifugio, STARE, CodTranslate, and Fluxa, plus automation and IoT sensor work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Book a Rifugio is currently a public demonstration environment for mountain-hut bookings; data and reservations there are illustrative.
- STARE is a published iPhone app for intentional sitting and one photograph at the end, with its own site and App Store listing.
- Fluxa is a conceptual, interactive tank-monitoring pilot site, with simulated data rather than connected field sensors.
- CodTranslate is a live, authenticated vehicle-document workspace with German and Austrian registration forms and Romanian document and contract generation.
- The existing Blog and Contact routes must keep working. The former Filosofie page is removed from the public studio site.
- The company homepage uses English. No invented clients, team size, results, or completed IoT deployments.

## Brand Commitments

The company name is Bitlads Software. Present it as a professional company, without one-person framing. The established Bitlads dark and yellow identity is recognizable but may be redesigned.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with real work and direct links.
2. Label concepts and demos accurately.
3. Make the company's range legible without suggesting every project is at the same stage.
4. Give potential customers an obvious route to contact the company.
