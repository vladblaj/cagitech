Atlas of Human Making

132 works, 63 countries, from a scratched piece of ochre 73,000 years old to a floor of porcelain seeds. The test for inclusion was not beauty or fame but consequence: after this object existed, something about how humans see, build, believe or organise themselves was different.

Pictures are loaded live from Wikipedia, so the atlas needs an internet connection to show them; where an image cannot be found you'll see a card with the work's name instead. Dates for ancient works are approximate and often contested. Coordinates mark where a work was made or stands — the panel tells you where it lives now, which for a great many of these is a different continent from the one that made them.
