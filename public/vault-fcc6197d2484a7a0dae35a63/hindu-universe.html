Reading the map

Distance from the centre is weight in living worship. Vishnu, Shiva and Krishna sit near the middle; the further out a figure lies, the smaller their part in what Hindus actually do today. Dot size says the same thing. It is not a ranking of importance in scripture — several of the most important Vedic gods sit well out from the centre, because their temples are gone and their mantras are not.
